# Security

Rewards are stock, so a way to claim a receipt that is not yours is the bug we care about most.

Send it to hello@usestockback.xyz with `security` in the subject line. Include what you did, what happened, and the claim reference if there is one. If it concerns funds at risk, tell us before you tell anyone else, and give us a way to reach you back.

In scope: the claims API, the fingerprinting of receipts, the keeper, the shelf data. Out of scope: the tokenized shares themselves, which are issued by a third party, and anything on Robinhood Chain we do not deploy.

We do not run a bounty programme yet. We do credit reporters in the changelog if they want it.
