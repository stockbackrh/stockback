# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- The shelf: 38 brands, rates, proof routes, settlement medians.
- The scanner: receipts are read in the browser, merchant and total parsed on the page, claims turned away with a reason.
- Landing with the receipt calculator and the settlement chart.
- Docs, FAQ, contact.

## 0.2.0

- Wallet connect on Robinhood Chain, no SDK.
- Claims API: wallet signature verified, reward recomputed from the shelf, one receipt one reward, three a day.
- Live settlement medians on the shelf.
- Token page, terms, privacy.

## 0.3.0

- Settlement keeper: ETH to USDG to the brand's stock token on Uniswap v3, delivered to the claimant in one transaction.
- Dark theme with the StockBack mark, blob background, hero video.
- Mobile polish, robots, sitemap, 404.
