How it works

Four steps, and one number that tells you whether the fourth of them actually happened.

  1. 01

    Photograph it, or forward it

    Shoot the till receipt in the app, or forward the order email to receipts@usestockback.xyz from the address you signed in with. Both routes end at the same place: a claim with a merchant, a date and a total. Paper from the till, confirmations from your inbox, renewals from the subscriptions you already pay for.

  2. 02

    It gets read, not reviewed

    A model pulls the merchant, the date and the total, checks that the paper is paper and that the email came from the merchant's own domain, then matches the merchant to its ticker. No queue and no person deciding whether your receipt looks right. A claim is accepted or turned away in the time it takes to read it, and if it is turned away you are told why.

  3. 03

    The reward is priced

    The brand's rate, applied to the total, converted at market into the brand's own tokenized share. A $186 Costco run at 2% is $3.73 in COST. Capped at $20 a receipt, or $100 when the wallet holds $STOCKBACK. The share is the same one anyone else can buy on Robinhood Chain, issued by a licensed tokenized-securities provider, and it sits in a wallet only you control.

  4. 04

    Watch it settle

    From the moment a claim is accepted, a clock runs on the reward until the shares are in your wallet. That time is printed on every brand on the shelf, live, as the median of the last hundred rewards. If it ever reads in days rather than minutes, you will see it before you scan, not after.
